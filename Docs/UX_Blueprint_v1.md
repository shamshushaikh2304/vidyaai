# AryaLearn UX Blueprint v1

## Product Vision

AryaLearn combines:

* Netflix-style discovery for students
* Curriculum confidence for parents
* AI-powered personalized learning

Students experience learning journeys.

Parents experience curriculum coverage and progress tracking.

---

# User Types

## Student

Goals:

* Continue learning quickly
* Discover new content
* Complete episodes
* Track achievements

---

## Parent

Goals:

* Verify curriculum coverage
* Track student progress
* Monitor learning consistency
* Ensure board/class alignment

---
